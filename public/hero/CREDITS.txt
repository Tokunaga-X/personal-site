West Lake hero illustration
===========================

An original illustration of Hangzhou's West Lake, created for this site.
Peach blossoms and lotus in the foreground, willows, tourist boats,
lakeside pavilions, and a pagoda on a wooded hill.

Files:
- west-lake-illustration.webp
- west-lake-illustration.avif
- west-lake-illustration-mobile.webp
- west-lake-illustration-mobile.avif

The desktop files are the full illustration at 3840×2160, encoded from a
5120×2880 master. The mobile files are a 1620×2880 crop of that master,
with the same portrait framing as before.
